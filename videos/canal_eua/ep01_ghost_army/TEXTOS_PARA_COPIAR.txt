===== TITLE =====
How American Artists Fooled the German Army With Rubber Tanks

(alternativas: "The Secret Army of Artists That Fooled Hitler" / "They Fooled the Germans With Rubber Tanks | The Ghost Army of WWII")

===== DESCRIPTION =====
In 1944, a secret American unit of artists, designers and sound engineers went to war armed with inflatable tanks, sound trucks and fake radio messages. Their mission: fool the German army. They did it again and again, from Normandy to the Rhine, and for more than fifty years almost nobody knew. This is the true story of the Ghost Army.
